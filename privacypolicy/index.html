Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Games") developed and published by Process Games / Hasan Büber ("we," "our," or "us").

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the information of our players ("you"). This policy outlines how data is collected, used, and safeguarded across our entire portfolio of Games.

1. Information We Collect

Our Games do not require user account registration, names, or direct contact information to play. However, non-personal and device-level data may be collected automatically through third-party SDKs integrated into our Games:

  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, hardware specs, operating system version, IP address, language preferences, network connection type, and unique advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Usage & Game Data: Gameplay progress, achievements, session lengths, in-game preferences, and system crash/performance logs.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic location (country or city level) derived from your IP address.

2. How We Use Information

We and our service providers use the collected data to:

  • Ensure proper functionality and performance of our Games.
  • Serve tailored (personalized) or non-personalized in-game advertisements.
  • Mediate ad networks to display high-quality ads.
  • Track analytics to identify bugs, improve gameplay balance, and release updates.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.

3. Third-Party Services & SDK Privacy Policies

Our Games integrate trusted third-party SDKs for ad mediation, advertising, analytics, and platform services. These partners collect and process device identifiers according to their own privacy practices.

You can review the individual Privacy Policies of our SDK providers below:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay (Mediation) & Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

4. Children's Privacy

Our Games are designed for general audiences and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiers from children. If you believe we have inadvertently gathered data from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

5. Data Security

Data transmitted between your device and our third-party service partners is protected in transit using industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL). While we apply commercial best practices to protect data, no method of digital transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. User Choices & Data Opt-Outs

  • Ad Tracking Options: You can reset your device's Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android settings: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Data Deletion Requests: Since we do not maintain individual user accounts or personal profiles on private servers, clearing your device's advertising ID or uninstalling the Game removes active data association. For specific data removal inquiries, please contact us via email.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Games, tech stack, or legal requirements. The latest version will always be accessible via our store listings and official channels.
